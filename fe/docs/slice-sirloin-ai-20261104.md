## Final prompt

Use case: product-mockup. Create a photorealistic square catalog photo of SLICE SIRLOIN: raw very lean beef sirloin cut into thin, broad, elongated slices, arranged neatly overlapping in two rows in a plain rectangular white food tray. Most important requirement: the entire flesh is uniformly deep fresh beef red with visible fine natural muscle grain, ABSOLUTELY NO WHITE MARBLING, no white spiderweb veins, no white fat flecks, no thick fat edges, no fatty bands, no injected fat. Fully trimmed red meat only, not wagyu and not meltique. The slices should look thin and flexible, about 2mm, not thick steak medallions. Clean natural red meat texture with subtle fresh moisture, realistic soft folds where slices overlap. Camera overhead with a slight natural angle, centered tray occupying 75–80 percent of the frame, all tray edges visible and charcoal slate around it. Input image is a STYLE REFERENCE ONLY for the plain white tray, dark charcoal slate background, soft upper-left studio light and premium catalog framing; do not copy its fatty beef subject. Restrained commercial food photography, natural colors, realistic detail. Opaque square image. No salt, seasoning, garnish, herbs, vegetables, utensils, hands, branding, text or watermark.

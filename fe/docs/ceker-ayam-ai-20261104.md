## Prompt

Use case: product-mockup. Asset type: square premium raw poultry catalog photograph for Tatsuo Beef. Generate a NEW product photo of raw boneless chicken feet (ceker ayam tanpa tulang), fully deboned and cleaned, nails removed, recognizable splayed toe-shaped soft skin pieces with natural deboning slits, no rigid bones protruding. Arrange about 8–10 pieces in a neat low mound on a plain round white ceramic plate. Natural pale ivory to faint pink skin, realistic fine pebbled skin texture and subtle fresh moisture. No blood, no cooking, no sauce. Reference image is STYLE ONLY: match its dark charcoal textured slate surface, soft directional studio light, overhead camera view, restrained premium food-catalog treatment and plate framing; replace its chicken breast subject entirely with boneless chicken feet. Center plate fully visible with dark slate border on all sides, subject occupies about 65–75% of square image. Anatomically plausible poultry pieces, photorealistic, appetizing clean commercial product photography. No herbs, garnish, props, utensils, text, logos, watermark. Opaque background.
